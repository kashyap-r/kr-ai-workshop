# Performance Management and Extended Leave

Employees on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) retain their contractual and statutory leave rights.

However, fictional company policy requires:
- HR review for extended leave of more than 10 consecutive working days during an active PIP.
- Department-head approval when an extended leave overlaps a critical delivery period.
- Leave should not be described as denied solely because the employee is on PIP unless the source record explicitly says it was denied.

This policy is intended to create realistic multi-document retrieval cases.

### Contract precedence
Where an employment contract explicitly grants a more favorable entitlement, the contract-specific entitlement applies to that employee. Where a contract requires additional approval, that approval is required.

### PIP / performance management
A performance-management note may require HR or executive review for extended leave. It does not remove statutory or contractual leave rights.

## Examples
- Sweden employee working in India for two weeks -> Swedish policy.
- India employee working in Germany for two weeks -> German policy.
- Germany employee working in India for two weeks -> Indian policy.
- India employee permanently transferred to Germany -> German policy after transfer date.
