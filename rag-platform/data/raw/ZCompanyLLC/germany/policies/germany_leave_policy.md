# Germany Leave and Work Policy

**Fictional policy for the RAG demo.**

## Annual leave
- Standard annual leave entitlement: **28 working days per calendar year**.
- Carry-forward limit: **5 days**.
- Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days requires manager and HR approval.
- Existing business-critical blackout periods may require additional approval.

## Parental / maternity leave
- Fictional maternity leave entitlement: **6 months**.
- Fictional paternity/partner leave entitlement: **8 weeks**.
- Parental leave must be administered using the employee's applicable jurisdiction.

## Sick leave
Paid sick leave subject to company policy and applicable German statutory rules.

## Remote work
- Temporary remote work outside the employee's base location must be recorded with People Operations.
- Jurisdiction for leave and holiday questions follows the global jurisdiction policy.

## Country-specific rule
Employees based in Germany normally use this policy.
