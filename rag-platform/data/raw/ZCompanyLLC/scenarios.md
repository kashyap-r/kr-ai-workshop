# RAG Demonstration Scenarios

These are manual demo questions, not a golden/evaluation dataset.

1. Can Rahul Mehta take three weeks of annual leave in September while he is temporarily working in Germany?
2. Which maternity leave policy applies to a Sweden-based employee temporarily working in India?
3. How many annual leave days does Priya Shah receive, and is that different from the standard India policy?
4. Why might Rohan Kapoor's December leave request require additional review?
5. Can Jonas Wagner use German leave rules while temporarily assigned to Sweden?
6. What happens to leave jurisdiction after a permanent transfer from India to Germany?
7. Which holiday calendar should apply to an India-based employee temporarily working in Germany?
8. Does being on a PIP remove an employee's right to annual leave?
9. What additional approval does Oskar Berg need for very long leave?
10. Compare annual leave and maternity leave entitlements across Sweden, Germany, and India.
