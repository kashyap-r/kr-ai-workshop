# Fictional Global HR RAG Demo Corpus

This corpus is intentionally fictional and exists only for demonstrating a production-oriented RAG pipeline.

## Jurisdiction rules

1. Every employee has a **base location**.
2. For employees whose base location is Germany or India, the policy of the **host country** applies when they are temporarily working from another country.
3. **Sweden is the headquarters. Swedish HQ policy follows Swedish employees globally**. A Sweden-based employee remains under Swedish HQ policy even while temporarily working in Germany, India, or another country.
4. A permanent transfer changes the employee's base location and therefore changes the normal policy jurisdiction.
5. Contract-specific clauses can provide an employee-specific entitlement or approval requirement.
6. Performance/PIP notes can impose additional approval requirements for extended leave; they do not automatically cancel statutory leave.
7. Holiday calendars are country-specific and should be interpreted using the employee's applicable jurisdiction under the rules above.

## Dataset composition

- Sweden: 10 employees
- Germany: 5 employees
- India: 10 employees
- Country policy documents
- Country holiday lists
- Employee profiles
- Employee leave records
- HR notes for selected employees
- Employment contracts in PDF format
- Cross-country assignment/travel cases

All names, policies, records, and contracts are fictional.
