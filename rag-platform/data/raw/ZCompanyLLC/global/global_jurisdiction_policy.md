# Global HR Policy Jurisdiction

## Purpose
This fictional policy defines which country policy governs leave and holiday questions.

### Base-location rule
An employee normally follows the HR policy of their permanent base location.

### Host-country exception
For a Germany- or India-based employee who is temporarily working from another country, the host country's leave and holiday rules apply for the duration of the temporary assignment, unless a contract or assignment letter states otherwise.

### Sweden headquarters exception
Sweden is the company headquarters. A Sweden-based employee remains governed by Swedish HQ policy while temporarily working outside Sweden. The host-country exception does not override Swedish HQ policy for Sweden-based employees.

### Permanent transfer
A permanent transfer changes the employee's base location. The employee then follows the policy of the new base country from the effective transfer date.

### Contract precedence
Where an employment contract explicitly grants a more favorable entitlement, the contract-specific entitlement applies to that employee. Where a contract requires additional approval, that approval is required.

### PIP / performance management
A performance-management note may require HR or executive review for extended leave. It does not remove statutory or contractual leave rights.

## Examples
- Sweden employee working in India for two weeks -> Swedish policy.
- India employee working in Germany for two weeks -> German policy.
- Germany employee working in India for two weeks -> Indian policy.
- India employee permanently transferred to Germany -> German policy after transfer date.
